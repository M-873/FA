# Royalty-Free Background Music Directory

Place your licensed or royalty-free audio tracks (`.mp3`, `.wav`, `.m4a`, `.aac`) in this directory.

The weekly video reel workflow will automatically scan this folder, select a track at random, and mix it into the AI-generated video reel at low volume (-20dB).

Recommended sources for royalty-free background music:
- YouTube Audio Library (Free to use under Creative Commons / YouTube License)
- Pixabay Music
- Incompetech (Kevin MacLeod)
- Free Music Archive
